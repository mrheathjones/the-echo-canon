# The Founding Trio Timeline

Early childhood:
Nathan and Sarah are friends from babyhood.

3rd grade:
Adrian moves to town and is welcomed into Nathan and Sarah's friendship.

Youth/adolescence:
The trio becomes inseparable. Adrian develops radical ideas about human suffering, structure, uncertainty, and difference. Sarah and Nathan push back, causing Adrian to feel like an outsider for the first time.

Young adulthood:
Nathan and Sarah fall in love. Adrian loves Sarah too but respects her choice. The trio remains close.

Marriage and Abby:
Nathan and Sarah marry. Adrian remains close and becomes Abby's godfather.

Sarah's death:
Nathan's reckless pursuit leads to Sarah's death. Abby is very young. Nathan is devastated. Adrian is devastated and loses both a dear friend and the secret love of his life.

After death:
Adrian introduces Nathan to the secret plan/framework that becomes the Accord. Nathan, seeing that Adrian was right about some things, joins as co-builder, not disciple.

Accord formation:
Nathan gives the movement humanity and public trust. Adrian provides architecture, philosophy, and systems.
