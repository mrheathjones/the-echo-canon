# Zoe

Zoe is a core friend and natural leader.

Family:
Zoe is an only child. Her mother Claire died a few years ago. The exact cause should remain largely untouched because it does not define Zoe. Her father Daniel is in the military and used to be deployed often. After Claire died, he took a lower-rank trainer role to be home more.

Grandparents and Maya's family helped a lot. Zoe sometimes has dinner at Eli or Mason's house when Daniel is away and grandparents are traveling.

Daniel:
Daniel trains Zoe “like a little soldier,” which Zoe actually likes because it gives structure and brings her closer to him. He is not a harsh “call me sir” stereotype. He values structure and says, “We play the game with the cards we've been dealt.” He wants Zoe to let loose more. His line “Slow progress is better than no progress” becomes part of Zoe's inner monologue.

Aunt Lacey:
Zoe's favorite aunt is Lacey, Daniel's sister. Lacey is a journalist, traveler, cool aunt, nonjudgmental listener, and safe place after Zoe lost her mother. Zoe wants to be like Lacey. Daniel loves their relationship but worries Lacey is flighty and unreliable.

Core Want: control.
False Belief: if I don't take charge, everything falls apart.
Weakness: improvising and asking for help.
Strength: leadership, strategy, protection, courage.

Personality:
Smart, capable, tactical, strategic, natural leader, straight-A student, high standards, protective. When there is a scavenger hunt, she forms a squad, delegates, and executes.

Flaw:
Her plans are often good but fail because her expectations on deliverables are unrealistic.

Relationship with Maya:
Zoe and Maya are chosen sisters. Zoe protects Maya from the outside world. Maya gives Zoe space to be exactly who she is without pretense.

Relationship with Lucas:
Lucas resists being told what to do. Zoe's plans sometimes conflict with Lucas's ideas. Zoe struggles to stray from the plan, creating tension. Zoe prefers a small manageable circle; Lucas is a people person, which annoys her.
