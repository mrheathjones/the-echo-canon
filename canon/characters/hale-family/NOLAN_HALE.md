# Nolan Hale

Nolan Hale is 19 and Emma's twin.

Core role: Lucas's complicated mirror and warning.

Traits:
- impulsive
- reactive
- social
- not especially emotional outwardly
- sometimes angry
- defiant toward authority
- cool, confident, well-liked
- charismatic
- master manipulator
- highly adaptable
- chameleon-like in social settings
- outdoorsy and athletic
- skateboards, hikes, hangs with friends
- cannot sit still for long; if home too long he drums up problems for dopamine

Core Want: freedom.
Core wound: feeling like the black sheep/problem child.
Growth arc: manipulation is not connection.

Exceptional ability:
Nolan understands people and can use that to his advantage. He often gets what he wants without people feeling like they got taken. This makes him a natural agent or recruiter type.

Cost of flaw:
Nolan has burned bridges his whole life. Part of his growth is realizing manipulation has a cost that is often paid by the people around him.

Relationship with Lucas:
Lucas admires Nolan's coolness, confidence, humor, popularity, and strong sense of self. Lucas also does not want to become Nolan's negative side: argumentative, toxic, manipulative, reckless.

Nolan often has a “do as I say, not as I do” older-brother attitude. He may tell Lucas not to argue with Mom and Dad. Lucas replies, “But you argue with them all the time.” Nolan answers, “Yeah, that's me. You shouldn't do it.”

Nolan's parenting resentment:
When Lucas does something Nolan would have gotten in trouble for but does not, Nolan throws it in the parents' faces: “If I had done that...” It is not about wanting Lucas punished. It is about how Nolan views his own upbringing.

Relationship with Gramps:
Gramps is Nolan's safe harbor. He sees the good in Nolan, expects better, does not excuse him, and rarely gives speeches.

Accord connection:
Nolan can plausibly align with the Accord because he agrees with some of its ideals. He is drawn to structure, relief, being seen as powerful rather than problematic, and being told his force needs direction.
