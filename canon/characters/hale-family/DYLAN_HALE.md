# Dylan Hale

Dylan Hale is 8.

Core Want: possibility.
Role: keeper of possibility.
Theme: stay yourself.
Strength: emotional courage.
Weakness: assumes people mean well.
False Belief: if people understood each other, they would get along.

Dylan wants fun, adventure, and possibility. He believes that if you believe something and go for it, maybe it can happen.

He is a little apprehensive about some things, but deep down he is very brave.

Dylan knows exactly who he is at 8. He knows what he likes and does not like and can politely decline. He is matter-of-fact, tells it like it is with humor, and is not trying to be mean.

Social style:
Dylan is more reserved in public until he assesses the room and catches the vibe. At home he talks constantly because home is safe. He is not afraid to answer or ask questions at school, but he warms up first.

Interests:
- science
- art
- dinosaurs
- stars
- black holes
- parallel universes
- rotating interests/hyperfixations

Relationship with David:
Dylan and David share curiosity and “pointless” information. David gets pulled into Dylan's rotating interests and learns along with him.

Family joke:
Rachel says Lucas looks like David but acts like Rachel, while Dylan looks like Rachel and acts like David.

Relationship with Lucas:
Dylan sticks up for Lucas when Nolan gets on him, which puts Dylan in Nolan's crosshairs. Dylan also asks for things Lucas wants but will not ask for:
Dylan: “Dad, can Lucas have a popsicle?”
Dad: “Lucas can ask for himself.”
Dylan: “Well I want one too.”

Dylan is protected not because he is weak, but because he is authentic. The family is protecting the last person who has not started compromising himself.
