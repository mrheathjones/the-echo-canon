# Sarah Whitmore

Sarah Whitmore is The Heart of the founding trio.

Role:
- childhood friend of Nathan
- friend of Adrian after he moved to town in 3rd grade
- emotional center of the trio
- Nathan's wife
- Abby's mother
- Adrian's closest friend and secret love

Sarah was loved by both men. Adrian loved her but never acted on it. Nathan and Sarah knew, and the three handled it with maturity and mutual respect. It was not a scandal or melodramatic love triangle.

Function in trio:
Sarah grounded Nathan when he was reckless and challenged Adrian when he became ideological. She held the friendship together and made both men more human.

Death:
Sarah died when Abby was very young as a direct but unintended consequence of Nathan's reckless pursuit.

Impact:
Her death permanently changes Nathan and Adrian.
- Nathan internalizes: I caused this.
- Adrian systematizes: this should never have been allowed to happen.

Sarah becomes the absence around which the Accord forms.
