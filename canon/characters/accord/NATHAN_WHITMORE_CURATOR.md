# Nathan Whitmore - The Curator

Nathan Whitmore is The Curator.

Age: 47.

Appearance reference: approachable, intelligent, professional, warm, trustworthy, put-together, non-threatening. He looks like a person parents trust, kids listen to, and board members respect.

Public role:
Nathan is publicly in charge of the Accord. Everyone knows it and they are correct. He is the face, leader, guide, and movement-builder.

Personal history:
Nathan was childhood friends with Sarah and Adrian. Nathan and Sarah knew each other from babyhood; Adrian joined the group after moving to town in 3rd grade.

Nathan and Sarah fell in love and married. They had one daughter, Abigail “Abby” Whitmore.

Founding wound:
Nathan had enormous potential and, in pursuit of that potential, made a reckless decision that directly but unintentionally led to Sarah's death. Sarah died when Abby was very young, barely old enough to remember her.

Nathan was devastated, broken, and hollowed out by guilt.

Core Wound: I caused this.
Core Belief: potential is too precious to leave to chance.
Core Gift: sees potential in people almost instantly.
Fatal vulnerability: cannot fully forgive himself.

Relationship with the Accord:
Nathan does not join the Accord because he wants power. He joins because Adrian's framework gives meaning to unbearable grief. The Accord becomes a way to ensure Sarah's death was not meaningless.

Relationship with Abby:
Nathan loves Abby deeply. He does not intentionally indoctrinate her, but expects her to keep up appearances and live within Accord expectations. His desire to protect her can become pressure.

Future arc:
Nathan may begin healing, rediscovering the value of imperfection, uncertainty, and messiness. This creates eventual tension with Adrian.
