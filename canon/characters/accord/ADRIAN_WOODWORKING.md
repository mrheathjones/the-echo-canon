# Adrian Mercer - Woodworking And Carvings

LOCKED: Adrian whittles/wood-carves.

He carves when:
- he needs to calm down
- he needs to think
- he is grieving
- he is planning
- he is solving problems

Carving helps him think clearly. He likes making something from nothing, or more accurately, revealing shape from rough material.

Symbolic meaning:
Adrian sees people the way he sees wood: rough, full of possibility, capable of becoming something meaningful through patience, precision, and intentional shaping.

Danger:
He may believe the figure was always inside the wood and he only revealed it. Lucas may eventually challenge this: what if the wood did not want to become that?

Accord campus:
Adrian's carved figurines appear throughout Accord spaces: owls, foxes, turtles, ravens, bears, and other small carvings in gardens, shelves, meeting spaces, libraries, and pathways.

Most members think they are decorations. They are not. They are Adrian's fingerprints on the campus.

Sarah's knife:
Sarah gave Adrian his first carving knife. He still has it, treasured though no longer used.
