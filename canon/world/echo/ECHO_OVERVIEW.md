# The Echo - World Overview

The Echo is a hidden layer of reality overlaying the normal world. It is not a separate dimension, timeline, or alternate universe. It exists alongside everyday reality, filtered out by most minds.

People encounter traces of the Echo as intuition, deja vu, dreams, strange familiarity, unexplained feelings, emotional weather, or flashes of something that should not be there.

Core rule: The Echo is objectively real, but perception of it is shaped by the perceiver.

Earlier versions framed neurodivergence as direct magic. That was rejected as too close to “disability as secret superpower.” Current canon is subtler:
- Lucas's mind filters less, so he perceives more raw Echo.
- This is a burden as much as an advantage.
- The true power is Attunement, which is available to everyone and earned through growth.
- Neurodivergence can create a different relationship to perception, but it is not a cheat code or proof that a person is better.

Echo manifestations can be born from accumulated experiences, emotions, memories, perceptions, relationships, repeated human behavior, and beliefs.
