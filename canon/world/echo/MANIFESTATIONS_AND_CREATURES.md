# Echo Manifestations And Creature Logic

Known manifestation ladder:
1. Impressions - subtle sensations, feelings, hints, deja vu.
2. Auras - visible distortions, emotional residue, environmental changes.
3. Orbs - semi-formed entities or concentrated emotional phenomena.
4. Manifested Creatures - fully formed entities that may be helpful, harmful, neutral, territorial, curious, or mysterious.
5. Great Echoes - rare, powerful entities that influence environments and perception itself.

Development note: Avoid letting the tier system become a Pokemon-style collection ladder. Escalation should mean perceiving more truth and handling deeper responsibility, not merely “bigger monsters.”

Echo creatures can embody emotions and pressures. They should not exist only as enemies to fight. They can be:
- fear given shape
- grief that has learned to hide
- curiosity wandering loose
- anger seeking a target
- loneliness becoming territorial
- joy becoming contagious
- suppressed selfhood trying to survive

Graphic novel opportunity: creatures should be visually memorable and emotionally readable.
