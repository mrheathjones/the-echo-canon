# The Gray And Hollows

The Gray is the visible deadening caused by the Accord's Quieting/flattening influence.

Where the Accord's flattening takes hold, the Echo does not become healthy. It becomes still. Color drains. Echo Weather dies down. Manifestations weaken or vanish.

To most people, the Gray feels calm, focused, stable, and fine.

To Lucas and other perceivers, it appears as a spreading dead zone.

Hollows are people who have been flattened enough that their personal Echo has gone dim or gray. They may appear high-functioning, agreeable, focused, and easier to manage, but something essential is missing.

Important nuance: If the Gray looks too much like obvious zombification, the Accord stops being tempting. The Gray should be subtle at first. The relief should be real before the cost becomes visible.

The Gray is one of the strongest cover images and marketing hooks: “a beloved adult is quietly turning kids gray, and only one boy can see it.”
