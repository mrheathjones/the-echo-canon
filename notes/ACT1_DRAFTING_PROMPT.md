## ACT 1 MAP (beats in order)

**1. Opening image — the Echo *miss* (pond / Eli's Barn).** Establish flood + friends + flaw in motion, no tour. Lucas catches an Impression, takes the **flattering** read, acts, and is **wrong** at a small social cost. Banks the anti-gifted-seer design from page one. No verbs on the page; he feels it, doesn't diagnose it.

**2. Ordinary world — the Hale household (the warmth bank).** Make the house a place you'd grieve. Contains the **Hale dinner** (gate scene) and the **store run** (already drafted — the brothers' two-hander; sits adjacent). Plant: Nolan **adrift** (recent HS grad, no next step — the vulnerability Devon exploits); the dimming; Gramps seeing Nolan's good; Lucas as the kid who *argues/explains* vs Nolan who *jokes/controls*. Mess, not system.

**3. Catalyst — the "double door."** The same threshold opens for both brothers, different tracks: Lucas + the four friends get the kids' **AIP** invite; Nolan announces he's joining the Accord **young-adult track** via **Devon Cross**. Nolan joining reads to the family as **relief** (the adrift kid finally has direction); *only Lucas feels the wrongness.* Keep Devon magnetic/sincere — source the unease to **Nolan changing**, not to Devon.

**4. Debate — Lucas's unease sounds like jealousy.** He can't articulate it; to everyone (maybe himself) it reads as jealousy of Nolan / being the Little left behind. Hold both truths: he *is* misunderstood **and** he isn't fully right. His worry must be genuinely indistinguishable from jealousy on the surface, or the midpoint's "his certainty is the blade" loses its setup.

**5. Break into two — first day on campus.** Dropped mid-problem (no tour). Meet **Ben Carter** (sincere first-year mentor) and **Abby** (insider / B-plot lead); catch the **faint campus Gray** he can't place. The drafted **Abby seed** (`act2a_abby_seed.md`) slots here. End Act 1 with the threshold crossed: Lucas in, Nolan in (different track), the Gray glimpsed, Devon circling Nolan — the machine running, the deposit banked.

**Pacing watch:** richness *and* momentum. The dinner + store run must land *before* the catalyst, because they're the deposit the catalyst starts spending — but don't let the ordinary world sag.

## THE HALE DINNER FRAMEWORK (write this first — it's the gate)
If this hits the store-run register, Act 1 has a foundation. If it comes out a notch below, that's the signal to find the risk early.

**Jobs, ranked (every line serves one):**
1. **Bank the warmth** — make the table a place you'd grieve.
2. **Nolan magnetic and lovable** — funny, sharp, controls the room — and **lovable ≠ harmless** seeded once.
3. **The dimming** — the faintest wrongness in Nolan that Lucas catches and **dismisses**. Almost subliminal.
4. **"Own It," lived** — a real family reflex in action, never stated as a lesson.
5. **Lucas's position** — a Little; argues/explains (vs Nolan's joke/control); inside-but-slightly-outside.

**A shape to write against (options, not a script):**
- **Cold-open in the chaos** — mid seat-argument or a roast already rolling. No "the Hales were a loud family." We learn them by being *in* it.
- **Roast volley** — Nolan starts it (canon: he usually does), pokes Lucas or Emma; establish control-the-room energy + the affection under the needling.
- **Warmth peak — the Jack food-text.** It's one of Jack's favorites; the table conspires on a teasing photo/caption ("sucks to suck" / "I ate yours"). Jack's seat is **already empty** (away at college) and the ritual is how they hold it. **Bank this hard — it pre-echoes the seat that empties later.**
- **The "Own It" beat** — best option: **Nolan's barb goes a hair too far** (canon allows it, rarely) and the family reflex makes him *own it*. Triple duty: dramatizes Own It, runs intent≠impact, keeps Nolan accountable-and-lovable. No one explains the value; it just operates.
- **The dimming (bury it)** — one micro-beat: Lucas catches something off in Nolan (color a hair low, a laugh half a second late, a flatness under the performance) and dismisses it — *probably the light, probably nothing.* Don't flag it.
- **The button — land on a threshold.** The truest button is a departure, where the **goodbye rule** operates (`AFFECTION_AND_GOODBYES.md`): someone peels off to leave, an easy "love you" in its natural slot — and **that is where the dimming sits.** Lucas catches Nolan's goodbye landing a half-second flat, or the "love you" a beat automatic, and dismisses it. The family law is shown *working* at the exact moment its future breaking is quietly loaded. (Alt: cleanup — *"Nolan notices"* the Littles struggle and quietly helps, unbought, while Lucas catches him looking at the table like he's already half-gone.)

**Plant/bank checklist (tie each to a payoff):** Nolan adrift → Devon's opening · the dimming → the A-plot · "Own It" → Lucas's arc + Nolan's intent≠impact · food-text/"your seat exists" → detonates when a seat empties · **the goodbye rule + "love you" slots → bank the family's spoken affection so a broken/half-meant goodbye detonates later** · Gramps sees Nolan's good → the reader's permission slip · Lucas argues/Nolan controls → the recoil + correction.

**Voice & guardrails:** narrator at 13 (feel, don't diagnose) · **mess, not system — NO Echo verbs on the page; the dimming is felt, not "read"** · zero-to-one thesis lines (ideally zero — let the warmth be the argument) · **warmth runs through teasing by default, but the Hales are not unsentimental** — sincerity lives in protected slots (leaving, hanging up, goodnight) and the hard goodbye rule; the ache comes from *specificity* plus the **contrast** between a table of barbs and a doorway "love you" that's meant, not from the narrator calling it precious (`AFFECTION_AND_GOODBYES.md`) · hold Nolan's simultaneity — lovable AND capable of damage, proven by the too-far barb + the owning.

**Pitfalls:** theme wearing a dinner (a scene that exists to deliver "family matters" — kill it) · the roster dump (introducing seven people + ages via narration — reveal through action) · Nolan too nice (no engine) or already-sinister (dimming too hard) · all-teasing-no-sincerity (sitcom) **or** all-sincerity (a different family) — hold the contrast · coming in under the store run because it's first in drafting order.
