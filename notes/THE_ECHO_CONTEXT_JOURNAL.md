## 1. PROJECT IN ONE PARAGRAPH
"The Echo" — an **upper-middle-grade**, **illustrated-prose-hybrid** hidden-world fantasy series. Protagonist **Lucas Hale, 13**, neurodivergent (ADHD + dyslexia). The magic system is a way of *perceiving* (not power) and it doubles as Lucas's central flaw. The differentiator vs. typical MG: an **intact, loud, loving family** (the threat is routed through a beloved older brother, not an orphan's absence). Author = GitHub user **mrheathjones**. Repo (public): https://github.com/mrheathjones/the-echo-canon

## 2. LOCKED DECISIONS (all author-confirmed)
- **Category:** upper MG, not YA. Ages: Lucas 13; Dylan ~10; Nolan 19 (recent HS grad, adrift — Accord young-adult-track recruit); Emma 19; Jack ~20.
- **Format:** illustrated prose hybrid (prose = family/interiority/adult-tragedy; full-bleed art = the Gray, Echo Weather, manifestations).
- **POV:** close third, anchored on Lucas; rationed leans to others.
- **Magic verbs (perception, not power):** CATCH → READ → HOLD → NAME → CHOOSE. System = the theme "being misunderstood doesn't mean you're right" (that's the READ failure mode).
- **Resolution = Option B (reckoning):** naming reveals; a costly choice resolves, usually made by the afflicted person. Small Impressions resolve on true naming alone.
- **The Gray = intended-as-mercy:** the relief taken when you're named truly and you *decline* the costly choice. Threat is method, not malice. (Keeps the institution non-cartoonish.)
- **Friend-group = distributed engine, rotating lead chair**, de-overlapped on distinct axes:
  - Lucas = antenna (CATCH)
  - Maya **Sinclair** = reader-of-people (READ); axis = dread-of-the-wrong-choice
  - Zoe **Bennett** = driver (CHOOSE); axis = outcomes/events
  - Eli **Sullivan** = reader-of-systems (READ mechanism); axis = mechanism
  - Mason **Reed** = anchor/mover (HOLD); axis = performance-exhaustion
- **Book 1 spine (two plots = one):**
  - **A-plot (series engine):** **Nolan grays.** Lucas alone sees it; his certainty drives a rash "rescue" that backfires; he learns he *can't choose for Nolan*.
  - **B-plot (self-contained, teaches the verbs):** **Abby's suppressed self manifests**, resolved by Maya's true name + Abby's costly choice ("I don't know what I want to be").
  - Comps: Amari and the Night Brothers, Nevermoor; A Wrinkle in Time/Camazotz thematically.
- **Antagonistic force:** **Devon Cross** — sincere Accord young-adult-track recruiter; the "counterfeit brother" (Nolan-but-smoothed = Nolan-but-erased). Lucas's gift betrays him because the want is his.
- **Win ≠ calm.** Resolutions preserve chaos/aliveness; the gray retreats from the mess.
- **Nolan lovability guardrails (series-wide):** Gramps as reader's permission-slip; barbs are armor (show the cost to him); bank unbought kindness; let him be right/sharp; barbs punch up not down; as he grays he is *choosing*, not puppeted; Lucas's anger stays grief, never contempt; lovable ≠ harmless.

## 3. ENDGAME + SECOND CLOCK (decided this session, via external review)
- **Series endgame shape = SCHISM** (reject faction-breaks-bad and reject reform-as-shape). Schism = the magic system at institutional scale: the Accord splits between those who can hold not-knowing and those who can't; both sides sympathetic; no villain to defeat; the choice propagates person-by-person the way the Gray does.
- **Second clock (the engine after the founding grief resolves) — RECONCILED, lock before using:**
  Split the levers. **Adrian = architect (controls the Gray's design/philosophy). Nathan = scale/reach (controls how far it spreads).** Nathan's *grief* kept the Accord cautious ("we help those who come to us"); his *healing* makes him evangelical ("bring it to everyone") → that takes the foot off the brake and accelerates spread. So when Nathan begins to forgive himself, the Gray spreads faster, and "does Nolan come back?" becomes a **moving deadline traveling toward him.** The healed founder is *more* dangerous. (This preserves Adrian-as-architect, which the raw reviewer version broke.)
