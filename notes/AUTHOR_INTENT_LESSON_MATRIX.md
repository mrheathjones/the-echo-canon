# Author-Intent — Per-Character Lesson Matrix

> **AUTHOR-INTENT ONLY. NOT CANON.**
> This file is a private north-star for the author. It is deliberately **outside `canon/`**, so it
> does **not** roll into `MASTER_CANON_BIBLE.md` and is **not** part of the world's source of truth.
> **Do not quote any of these lines as prose. Do not hand this to a reviewer as canon.**
> A one-didactic-lesson-per-character list is a **preaching liability** — the exact thing the craft
> guardrails forbid (*"no character and no narrator delivers the lesson"*). It was moved here from
> `canon/THEMES.md` so the matrix can guide the author without ever surfacing on the page.

## How to use this
These are the truths each character is *built around* — the destination of their arc, never a line
anyone says. In the prose they must be **dramatized, not stated**. The truths already live, in
non-didactic form, where they belong:
- in each character's **dossier** (as wound / flaw / false-belief / arc), and
- at the thematic altitude in **`canon/THEMES.md` → LOCKED CORE THEMES** (e.g. "Relief is not the
  same as wholeness," "No one sees the whole picture," "love that survives imperfection").

If a scene ever *states* one of these, it has failed the don't-preach rule — cut the line and trust
the image.

## The matrix (private)
- **Lucas:** being misunderstood does not mean you are right.
- **Emma:** you do not have to earn your worth.
- **Nolan:** manipulation is not connection.
- **Rachel:** you cannot protect people from every struggle.
- **Jack:** growing up does not mean leaving home behind.
- **Dylan:** keep possibility alive without assuming everyone means well.
- **Maya:** peace does not come from perfect certainty.
- **Zoe:** control is not the same as safety.
- **Mason:** freedom requires vulnerability.
- **Eli:** understanding is not the same as control.
- **Nathan:** forgiveness cannot be replaced by prevention.
- **Adrian:** not every human problem can be engineered away.

## Note on Nathan & Adrian (the second clock)
Their two lines are the **Phase-2 end-lessons** of the second clock (see
`canon/00_SERIES_DECISIONS_LOCKED.md` decisions 8–9). That arc is grounded in the **engine canon**
— the lever-split, `POWER_STRUCTURE.md`, and the founders' dossiers — not in these one-liners, so
moving them out of canon costs the second clock nothing. They are a destination to *dramatize*
(Nathan needs his own on-page "garden"; Adrian never learns his), never a thesis to state.
